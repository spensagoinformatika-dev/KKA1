🎯 A. Definition

Simple Past Tense digunakan untuk menyatakan kegiatan atau peristiwa yang terjadi dan selesai di masa lampau.

Subject + Verb 2 + Object / Complement

Example: She visited her grandmother yesterday.

They played football last Sunday.

🧩 B. Sentence Patterns

Positive (+)
Subject + Verb 2 + ...
Negative (−)
Subject + did not + Verb 1 + ...
Interrogative (?)
Did + Subject + Verb 1 + ...?

Remember: Setelah did / did not, gunakan Verb 1.
